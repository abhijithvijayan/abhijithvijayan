### Abhijith Vijayan

I write **TypeScript** for browsers and **C/C++/Python** for things that aren't browsers: car head units, BLE sensors, scooter ECUs. Author of [web-extension-starter](https://github.com/abhijithvijayan/web-extension-starter) (2.2k★) and a handful of [npm modules](https://www.npmjs.com/~abhijithvijayan). Maintainer of the [Kutt.it](https://github.com/thedevs-network/kutt-extension) extension.

I film some of the tinkering on YouTube at [Practical Overkill](https://www.youtube.com/@PracticalOverkill) and occasionally [write](https://r.abhijithvijayan.in/synapse).
